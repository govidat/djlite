Since this covers a broad B2B SaaS suite (web pages + catalogue + demand planning + inventory decisions + eCommerce), the strongest names tend to lean into either **orchestration/unification** (since you're tying together many functions) or **flow/intelligence** (since the value-add is smart decisions across the chain).

A few directions with examples:

**Unification / platform feel**
- Stackmonk
- Unifyra
- Corestack Commerce
- OneChain
- Hubcommerce

**Flow / supply chain motion**
- Flowcart
- Demandflow
- Stockflow
- Chainwise
- Supplyloop

**Intelligence / decisioning angle**
- Forecastly
- Decidely
- Invently
- Plannix
- Demandix

**Short, brandable, domain-friendly**
- Shelfly
- Stockwise
- Catalo
- Mercanto
- Vendrly

A few practical notes before you commit to one:

- Check `.com`/`.io` availability and trademark conflicts early — many "-ly" and "-ify" names are heavily squatted.
- Since you're spanning catalogue, demand planning, inventory, *and* storefront, a name that doesn't pigeonhole you into just "e-commerce" or just "forecasting" will age better (e.g. **Stackmonk**, **OneChain**, **Chainwise** are more neutral than **Demandix** or **Catalo**).
